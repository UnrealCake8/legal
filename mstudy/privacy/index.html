🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MStudy (the "Application"), an independent student developer project, collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use the app. We are committed to protecting student privacy and keeping our data collection transparent.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the absolute minimum data required to deliver core functionality. Specifically, the Application collects:

  • Identity & Authentication Data: User's full name and email address (collected during registration or institutional login).
  • Academic Content: Study materials and resources explicitly shared or compiled within the app by users.
  • Academic Metadata: Information related to assignments, such as deadlines, titles, or status logs, to keep your dashboard synchronized.

We do not collect physical locations, phone data, cookies, or device hardware IDs.

2. How Your Information Is Used

The data collected is utilized strictly for operational study functions within the app. We use it to:

  • Provide user authentication and secure your individual profile.
  • Display your study materials, assignment structures, and schedules correctly.
  • Send critical application alerts or respond to direct feature support inquiries.

3. Data Sharing and Commercial Use

Your privacy is our priority. We do not sell, trade, rent, or monetize your personal information to third-party companies, data brokers, or advertisers in any capacity. Data stays within the Application framework to serve your study experience.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your user data as long as your account remains active. If you wish to delete your profile or clear your academic materials permanently from our system, you can submit a removal request using the developer contact information provided below.

5. Compliance and Institutional Use

Because MStudy functions as a tool for students at SES, users are expected to manage academic data responsibly. This independent project works in good faith but is not an integrated legal component of Google Classroom or the official institution infrastructure.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates. Any adjustments will be posted directly on this layout view. Continued use of the app signifies acceptance of any updated privacy terms.
