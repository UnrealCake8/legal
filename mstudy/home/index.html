MStudy

MStudy is a comprehensive, completely free productivity application designed exclusively for internal users enrolled at SES. The platform seamlessly synchronizes with your Google Classroom account, automatically retrieving crucial metadata regarding your assignments, tasks, and relevant course updates.

Engineered as an all-in-one academic hub, MStudy empowers you to efficiently manage your lecture notes, assignments, school timetables, campus events, and daily homework in one centralized location.
